## Quick Audit: 12 Conversion Killers

Check for these in order of impact. Any "yes" is an urgent fix.

| # | Issue | Check | Impact |
|---|-------|-------|--------|
| 1 | **Vague, corporate copy** | Can a stranger explain what you do after 5 seconds? | High — specificity is credibility |
| 2 | **Jargon** | Any mention of ETL, dbt, BI, API, data warehouse, pipeline? | High — alienates target audience |
| 3 | **Self-focused copy** | Count "We" vs "You" sentences. Does "We" dominate? | Medium-high |
| 4 | **Multiple competing CTAs** | More than one distinct action asked per section? | Up to 266% conversion loss |
| 5 | **Generic stock photography** | Any meaningless stock photos, icons, or illustrations? | 108% lift from removing |
| 6 | **No/weak social proof** | Missing testimonials, or only vague ones ("Great company!" — John D.)? | High |
| 7 | **Trying to close too soon** | Leading with pricing before establishing value? | High for high-ticket services |
| 8 | **Ad/page message mismatch** | Does the headline match whatever brought the visitor here? | Causes immediate bounce |
| 9 | **Slow load time** | Over 2 seconds? Each 1s delay = 7% conversion loss | Medium-high |
| 10 | **Poor mobile experience** | Single-column? Thumb-friendly buttons (44x44px min)? CTA visible without scroll? | 50%+ traffic is mobile |
| 11 | **Too many form fields** | More than 3–5 fields? | 120% better with fewer fields |
| 12 | **No risk reversal** | Missing a specific guarantee or value-in-advance promise? | 10% → 40% conversion with guarantee |

## Section-by-Section Audit

### Hero
- [ ] Headline is 6–12 words, benefit-driven, no jargon
- [ ] Subheadline specifies who, what, and a proof point (15–30 words)
- [ ] Exactly one primary CTA button in high-contrast color
- [ ] Friction-reducing microcopy beneath button
- [ ] Trust bar with logos or aggregate stat
- [ ] No generic stock photos — real visuals or none
- [ ] Passes the "grunt test" — can a visitor understand in 5 seconds?

### Logo/Social Proof Bar
- [ ] Appears immediately after hero
- [ ] Uses real client logos, partner badges, or "as featured in" logos
- [ ] Grayscale, clean horizontal layout

### Problem Section
- [ ] Uses reader's language, not consultant's
- [ ] 3–5 specific pain points in checklist format
- [ ] Quantifies the cost of inaction
- [ ] PAS framework applied (Problem → Agitate → Solution tease)

### Solution Section
- [ ] Framed as 3 key outcomes, not features
- [ ] Each outcome has icon + benefit + one-line description
- [ ] Includes CTA at section end

### How It Works
- [ ] Exactly 3 steps
- [ ] Each step is one sentence
- [ ] Step 1 is lowest possible commitment (free call)

### Case Study / Testimonials
- [ ] Challenge → Solution → Result format
- [ ] Specific metrics (hours saved, dollars found, timeline)
- [ ] Testimonials have full name, title, company, headshot
- [ ] CTA placed directly after this section

### About/Guide Section
- [ ] Professional photo included
- [ ] Shows empathy ("I've seen this problem...") + authority (credentials)
- [ ] Customer is positioned as hero, consultant as guide

### FAQ
- [ ] 4–5 questions in accordion format
- [ ] Addresses cost, timeline, technical ability, team size, differentiation
- [ ] Answers are concise and jargon-free

### Risk Reversal
- [ ] Specific guarantee, not vague "satisfaction guaranteed"
- [ ] Frames the discovery call as receiving value
- [ ] Any scarcity claim is genuine (capacity-based)

### Final CTA
- [ ] Restates transformation
- [ ] Paints the "after" picture
- [ ] Includes stakes of not acting
- [ ] Same CTA copy as hero
- [ ] Microcopy repeated

## CRO Benchmarks and Targets

| Metric | Industry Average | Good | Excellent |
|--------|-----------------|------|-----------|
| Landing page conversion rate | 3–6% (professional services) | 4–5% | 7%+ |
| Median all industries | 6.6% | — | 11.45%+ (top 10th percentile) |
| Speed to lead response | — | Under 30 min | Under 5 min (21x more likely to qualify) |
| Page load time | — | Under 3s | Under 2s |
| Mobile conversion rate | 2.19% | 3%+ | Closing the gap with desktop (4.03%) |

### High-Leverage CRO Additions
- **Interactive assessment/quiz** — converts up to 56% higher than static forms. A "Data Health Assessment" or "Spreadsheet Cost Calculator" is the single highest-leverage addition.
- **Personalization by traffic source** — dynamic headline swaps matching ad copy to page headline (+35% lift with Smart Traffic tools)
- **Ethical scarcity** — "We take on 3 new clients per month" (only if true)

## Social Proof Assessment

When Rapid Insights has limited clients, depth beats breadth:

### What to prioritize (in order)
1. **Detailed case studies** — Challenge → Solution → Result with specific metrics. Even 1 strong case study with a 73% conversion rate on its landing page.
2. **Video testimonials** — 45–90 seconds, Zoom quality fine. Up to 80% conversion lift.
3. **Technology partner badges** — dbt, Snowflake, Google Cloud, Fivetran. Borrows authority.
4. **Founder's personal brand** — origin story > corporate "About Us" for small firms
5. **Aggregate impact metrics** — "Helped clients save 2,000+ hours" or "Connected 150+ data sources"
6. **Thought leadership** — blog, guest posts, podcast appearances. 94% of CxOs consume thought leadership.
7. **"As Featured In" logos** — even local media or podcast appearances work. 3–6 grayscale logos.

### Testimonial Quality Check
Each testimonial must have:
- [ ] Full name (not initials)
- [ ] Job title
- [ ] Company name
- [ ] Specific result or metric
- [ ] Headshot (ideally)

Products with just 5 reviews have 270% higher purchase likelihood than those with none.

## Technical Performance

- [ ] Page loads under 2 seconds
- [ ] Images compressed (no unoptimized PNGs/JPGs)
- [ ] Meets Core Web Vitals (LCP, FID, CLS)
- [ ] Mobile-first design (single column, 44x44px buttons minimum)
- [ ] CTA visible without scrolling on mobile
- [ ] No off-page redirects for booking (Calendly embedded inline)
- [ ] Minimal JavaScript — no heavy scripts blocking render
